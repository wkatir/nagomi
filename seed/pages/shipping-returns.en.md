# Shipping and returns

## How your order reaches you
You place your order in the store and we confirm it with you on WhatsApp. Before it is final, we tell you the shipping cost, the estimated delivery date and the total. We confirm your order no later than the next day.

## Shipping cost
It depends on your address. We tell you on WhatsApp before you confirm, so you never pay for shipping you did not agree to.

## Batch and expiry
Before delivery, we tell you the batch and expiry date of every product. If you want to know before you order, just ask.

## Returns
You have 8 days from the day you receive your order to return a product, no questions asked. You can open the box to check it; to return it, the product must be unused and keep its safety seal, if it has one, its box and its labels. Message us on WhatsApp or by email: we give you a reference number and tell you where to send it. The return shipping is on you. We refund everything you paid within 15 days of your message.

## If something arrives wrong
If a product arrives damaged, expired or is not what you ordered, message us. We will replace it or refund you, and we cover the shipping, also within 15 days of your claim.
