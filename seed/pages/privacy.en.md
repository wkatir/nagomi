# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data we use and why. The company responsible for your data is listed at the end of this page.

## Data we use
- Your name, WhatsApp number and delivery address, to prepare, confirm and deliver your orders.
- Your email, if you give it to us.
- The products you buy, your payments, returns and the version of these texts you accepted.

## Why we use it
- To handle your orders, deliveries, returns and warranty claims, and to answer your questions.
- To meet legal obligations, such as keeping a record of every purchase.
- To send you offers on WhatsApp, only if you give us separate permission. You can withdraw it at any time by messaging us.

## Who we share it with
- The delivery service, only what it needs to bring your order.
- The services we use to operate: the store's hosting and WhatsApp, where we talk with you.
- Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it
We keep the record of your purchases for 10 years, as El Salvador's Consumer Protection Law requires. We use data for offers until you withdraw your permission.

## Your rights
You can ask to access, correct, delete, object to, port, erase or restrict the use of your data. Message us on WhatsApp or write to the email at the end of this page. It is free and we reply within 20 business days. If you are not satisfied, you can go to El Salvador's data protection authority, the Agencia de Ciberseguridad del Estado.

## Cookies and storage
We only keep in your browser what the store needs to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

- **Language** (cookie `PARAGLIDE_LOCALE`): remembers the language you chose. It lasts up to 400 days.
- **Cart** (local storage `eclalune:cart:v1`): keeps the products and quantities in your cart, with no personal data, until you empty it.
- **Cookie notice** (local storage `eclalune:cookie-notice:v1`): remembers that you read this notice.
- **Scroll position** (session storage): takes you back to the same spot when you go back, and is cleared when you close the tab.
- **Team session** (cookies `__Secure-better-auth`): only for the people who run the store; never used with customers.

If we ever use measurement or advertising tools, we will ask for your permission before turning them on, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.
