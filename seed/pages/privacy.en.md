# Privacy notice

This notice explains which personal data Nagomi processes, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights, under El Salvador's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley para la Protección de Datos Personales).

## Data controller
Your data is controlled by [Razón social pendiente], which runs the Nagomi online store in El Salvador. Tax ID (NIT): [NIT pendiente]. Address: [Dirección pendiente]. For any privacy matter, write to hola@nagomi.example.

## What data we collect
- **Account data:** name, email address and password (stored encrypted; we never see it).
- **Contact and delivery data:** phone number, department, municipality, address, landmark and the name of the person receiving the order.
- **Order data:** products, amounts, chosen payment method, order status and purchase history.
- **Payment data:** if you pay by card, the card details go straight to the payment gateway; Nagomi does not store them. For a bank transfer we only keep the reference to reconcile it.
- **Technical data:** your IP address and session data, to keep you signed in and protect your account.

## What we use it for
1. Creating and managing your account.
2. Processing, delivering and following up on your orders, including returns and warranty claims.
3. Answering your questions and complaints.
4. Meeting our legal, accounting and tax obligations.
5. Protecting the store against fraud and unauthorised access.
6. Only with your permission: sending you news and offers by email or WhatsApp.

## Legal basis
We process your data because it is needed to perform the purchase contract you enter into with us, to meet legal obligations and, for marketing messages, because you give us your express consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting your purchases.

## Who we share it with
Only with those we need to fulfil your order: the courier that delivers it, the payment gateway that processes your card and the technology providers that host the store. Each receives only the data it strictly needs. We never sell or rent your data. We may also hand it to an authority when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
Account data, for as long as your account is active. Order data, for as long as commercial and tax law requires. Marketing data, until you withdraw your permission. After that we delete or anonymise it.

## Your rights
You have the right to **access** your data, **rectify** it if it is inaccurate, **cancel** it when it is no longer needed, **object** to its processing and ask for its **portability** in a commonly used format. To exercise them, email hola@nagomi.example from your account's address, stating which right you want to exercise. We reply within 10 business days at most. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the competent data protection authority.

## Security
The store uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), passwords are stored encrypted and access to data is limited to the staff who need it for their work.

## Changes to this notice
If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this page with its date. We keep a record of the version you accepted when you created your account or placed an order.
