# About us

**Nagomi** (なごみ) is a Japanese word for harmony, calm and well-being. It is what we want you to feel every time you sit down at your desk.

We are an online store from El Salvador devoted to peripherals and accessories for your setup: mechanical keyboards, mice, desk mats, headphones, lighting and the details that make a desk feel like yours. We choose every product for how it works and how it looks, inspired by Japanese aesthetics: cherry blossoms, moonlit nights and violet light.

## What matters to us
- **Products that last.** Everything we sell comes with a warranty: 12 months on keyboards, mice, headphones and lighting, and 6 months on desk mats and accessories.
- **Clear prices.** What you see includes VAT, and you know the shipping fee before you pay.
- **Friendly help.** If you are not sure what to choose, we help you put together the setup you have in mind.

Alongside the brands we carry, we have our own brand, Nagomi, with desk mats, mice and accessories designed here.

*Your space, your style, your calm.*
